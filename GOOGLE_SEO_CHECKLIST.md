# FixMyDoor Google Search Console And SEO Checklist

Use this after every deployment to help Google find the correct FixMyDoor Services pages.

## 1. Confirm The Live Site

Open these URLs in a browser:

- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/sitemap.xml`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/robots.txt`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/manifest.json`

The sitemap should list the homepage and service pages. The manifest should show the `main-icon-v2` app icons.

## 2. Submit Sitemap

In Google Search Console:

1. Open the FixMyDoor property.
2. Go to `Sitemaps`.
3. Submit `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/sitemap.xml`.
4. Confirm the status becomes `Success`.

## 3. Inspect Important URLs

Use `URL Inspection` for:

- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/door-repair`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/entry-door-installation`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/furniture-repair`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/door-hardware`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/door-purchase`
- `https://www.fixmydoor.ca/buy-door-hardware`

If Google says the page is not indexed, click `Request Indexing`.

## 4. Understand Redirect And Canonical Warnings

Google may show:

- `Page with redirect`
- `Alternative page with proper canonical tag`

These can be normal when Google discovers old URLs, redirected URLs, trailing slash URLs, query URLs, or aliases.

Examples:

- `/door-repair/` should redirect to `/door-repair`.
- `/index.html` should redirect to `/`.
- Old service aliases should redirect to the clean service page.

Only the final clean canonical URLs need to be indexed.

## 5. Validate Fixes

After deployment:

1. Open the affected report in Search Console.
2. Click `Validate Fix`.
3. Wait for Google to recrawl.

This can take days or weeks. It is not instant.

## 6. Google Business Profile

For local Canadian reach, create and complete the Google Business Profile.

Add:

- Business name: `FixMyDoor Services`
- Category related to door repair, furniture repair, or handyman/service work.
- Address or service area.
- Phone number.
- Website URL.
- Opening hours.
- Service photos.
- Services offered.
- Reviews from real customers.

Google Business Profile is very important for local map results in Montreal and across Canada.

## 7. Keep Building SEO Value

To improve reach in Canada and internationally:

- Keep each service page unique.
- Add real project photos.
- Add approved customer reviews.
- Keep sitemap submitted.
- Keep business details consistent everywhere.
- Share service pages on Facebook, Instagram, X, Yellow Pages, and other listings.
- Ask satisfied customers for Google reviews.
- Avoid duplicate pages and thin content.
